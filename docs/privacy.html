Privacy Policy

Every calculation on this site runs inside your browser. The dimensions, prices and quantities you type are never sent to a server and never written to a database.

What we do not collect

  • No input data
  • No tracking cookies
  • No third-party analytics or ad scripts
  • No account, no email required

Browser extension

The extension runs offline on your machine. It requests no host permissions and does not read the pages you browse. The only permission is local storage, used to remember your last inputs — that data stays on your computer.

When you email us

A feedback email is something you choose to send. It contains your address and whatever you write. We use it only to reply and to correct the formulas. It is never shared or used for marketing.
